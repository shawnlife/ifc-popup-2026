What counts as what
Visits
One per page load. Reloading or reopening the site counts as a new visit.
Bounce rate
The share of visits where someone loaded the page and left without tapping anything else at all, not even switching tabs.
Session demand
Counts a session as opened only when someone taps into it: a title on the Schedule tab, or a "Speaking in" link on a speaker's profile. Scrolling past a card on the Sessions tab without tapping it is not counted.
Speaker interest
Counts each time a speaker's profile pop-up is opened, from any tab.
Filters used / Tabs used
Each time a Sessions-tab filter is changed, or a tab is switched.
LinkedIn clicked / Sponsor clicked
Each tap on a speaker's LinkedIn button, or a sponsor logo, credited to whichever speaker or sponsor it was.
Ticket clicks / ShawnLife clicks
Taps on the Get Your Tickets button, or the ShawnLife credit in the footer.
